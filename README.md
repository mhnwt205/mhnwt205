## 👋 About Me

I'm **Hoàng Minh Nhựt**, an early-career **Business Analyst & Developer** who enjoys turning unclear ideas into structured requirements, intuitive experiences, and working software.

My work connects two perspectives:

- **Business Analysis:** Understanding stakeholder needs, mapping workflows, writing clear specifications, and validating outcomes.

## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🏡 [Trọ Tốt TP.HCM — TimPhongTro](https://github.com/mhnwt205/TimPhongTro)

A full-stack room and mini-apartment listing platform focused on helping people find suitable accommodation in Ho Chi Minh City. The project connects **real rental workflows** with a practical web experience for renters, listing owners, and administrators.

**Highlights**

- 🔎 Search and filter by **location, rental price, room size, and amenities**.
- 🗺️ Explore room details with a photo gallery, **cost breakdown, and map**.
- 🏠 Manage user accounts, rental listings, and listing lifecycles.
- 🛡️ Support administrative review, reports, and content management.

**Stack:** `Astro` `TypeScript` `Hono` `Cloudflare Workers` `D1` `R2` `Leaflet`
